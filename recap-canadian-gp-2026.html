Race Recap · Round 5 · May 24, 2026

Antonelli Survives Montreal Mayhem for Four in a Row

Rain before the start, a McLaren gamble that backfired, and a Russell retirement that handed Kimi Antonelli another win.

Montreal never disappoints. Rain just before the start, a string of retirements and three virtual safety cars, and at the end of it all, Kimi Antonelli won for the fourth race running. Lewis Hamilton finished second for Ferrari, with Max Verstappen third.

McLaren's gamble

The rain left everyone guessing on tyres. Lando Norris jumped from third to the lead into the first corner, only for McLaren to bring both cars in at the end of lap 1 to switch from intermediates to slicks. It sent them both to the back.

Mercedes vs Mercedes

Antonelli led after the early stops, but polesitter George Russell got past him on lap 6, and the two swapped places several times. On lap 13, Oscar Piastri collided with Alex Albon, ending Albon's race and earning Piastri a ten-second penalty.

Russell's heartbreak

Then, on lap 30, Russell's car stopped on track with a power unit problem. The virtual safety car came out, Antonelli pitted, and he rejoined in the lead he'd keep to the end.

The retirements kept coming. Norris's gearbox failed on lap 40, Sergio Pérez's suspension broke a few laps later, and there were two more virtual safety cars after that.

Hamilton steps up

Hamilton had struggled for power, but he passed Verstappen for second on lap 62.

The race was cut from 70 laps to 68 after Arvid Lindblad's Racing Bulls broke down before the start, which meant an extra formation lap.

My take: Antonelli still has much to learn, especially in wheel-to-wheel racing. Russell showed stronger defense, cleaner overtakes and superior racecraft in their battle. Russell deserved to win, but an untimely mechanical failure ultimately cost him the race.
