Race Recap · Round 9 · July 5, 2026

Leclerc Wins Behind the Safety Car at Silverstone

Ferrari dominated the start, Antonelli's race fell apart, and a software glitch meant the race finished under the safety car.

Charles Leclerc won the British Grand Prix, Ferrari's second win of the season. But it ended in an odd way: the field crossed the line behind the safety car after a planned final-lap restart was cancelled.

Ferrari jump the Mercedes

Leclerc made the best start of anyone and took the lead from polesitter Kimi Antonelli. Lewis Hamilton passed Antonelli too, putting Ferrari first and second. Hamilton was hit with a five-second penalty for a possible false start, though.

Further back, Oscar Piastri broke his front wing after contact with Liam Lawson at Brooklands, and Alex Albon earned a ten-second penalty for hitting Oliver Bearman.

An umbrella on track

Antonelli passed Hamilton for second on lap 11 and started chasing Leclerc. A brief virtual safety car came out on lap 22, to clear an umbrella from the track. Only at Silverstone.

Hamilton and George Russell fought over third for most of the middle of the race, until Russell had to pit on lap 35 with a slow puncture. Hamilton then passed Verstappen on lap 38 for third.

Antonelli's race unravels

Antonelli was closing on Leclerc on hard tyres when he reported a problem on lap 41. It turned out to be a broken wheel shield, damaged on an aggressive kerb at Copse. An extra stop and a five-second track limits penalty dropped him to 15th and out of the points.

The strange finish

On lap 48, Max Verstappen spun at Stowe and retired, bringing out the safety car. Leclerc and Hamilton pitted for fresh softs, while Russell stayed out and moved into second. A restart was planned for the final lap, but race control cancelled it because of a software error, so the race finished under the safety car with the whole top ten within about three and a half seconds.
