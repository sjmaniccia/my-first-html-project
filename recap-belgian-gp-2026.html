Race Recap · Round 10 · July 19, 2026

Antonelli Hunts Down Leclerc at Spa

A first-lap Ferrari-Mercedes clash took Russell out, but Kimi Antonelli fought back past Charles Leclerc on the Kemmel Straight to win.

Kimi Antonelli won his sixth race of the year at Spa, but he had to work for it. He passed Charles Leclerc on the Kemmel Straight with ten laps to go and held on to win by under two seconds.

Lap 1 drama

Antonelli kept the lead into La Source, but Max Verstappen got a better exit and briefly led down to Eau Rouge. Antonelli slipstreamed back past on the Kemmel Straight, and Leclerc got by Verstappen too.

George Russell's race was over almost immediately. He lost electrical deployment after La Source and was passed by both Ferraris. Trying to fight back at Les Combes, Lewis Hamilton understeered into him, and Russell spun into the gravel and out. Hamilton got a five-second penalty, and the safety car came out.

Strategy shuffle

Racing resumed on lap 5. Oscar Piastri and Leclerc touched at Les Combes on lap 8, damaging Piastri's car, but the stewards took no action.

Verstappen pitted first, and Mercedes covered him by stopping Antonelli. A virtual safety car around lap 20 let Ferrari pit both cars cheaply, and Leclerc came out ahead. Hamilton's stop went wrong: he was released too early and hit a mechanic, who thankfully wasn't hurt.

Antonelli strikes back

Lando Norris led at half distance on a long stint on hard tyres until Leclerc passed him on lap 23. Antonelli struggled to match Leclerc's pace for a while, then made his move on the Kemmel Straight on lap 34 and managed the gap from there.

Drive of the day

Isack Hadjar started 21st and finished sixth on a three-stop strategy. Norris set the fastest lap on the final lap but couldn't catch him.
