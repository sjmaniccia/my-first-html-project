Race Recap · Round 3 · March 29, 2026

Antonelli Makes It Two as a Scary Crash Sparks a Rules Row

A safety car timed perfectly for Kimi Antonelli, and an Oliver Bearman crash that got everyone talking about the 2026 energy rules.

Kimi Antonelli is on a roll. He won at Suzuka from pole for two wins in a row, beating Oscar Piastri by nearly 14 seconds. But the race will be remembered for a crash, and the questions it raised.

A bad start for Mercedes

Piastri and Charles Leclerc got away brilliantly and ran first and second. Both Mercedes bogged down, with Russell dropping to fourth and Antonelli to sixth.

Russell fought back quickly, passing Lando Norris and then Leclerc in the first three laps, but he couldn't touch Piastri. Antonelli worked his way back to fourth by lap 10.

The crash that changed everything

The pit stops shuffled the order, and when Russell stopped on lap 20, Antonelli took the lead. On that same lap, Oliver Bearman crashed his Haas hard at Spoon.

He'd been closing fast on Franco Colapinto's Alpine, which was harvesting energy and running much slower at that point. Bearman had to swerve onto the grass to avoid him and lost control.

The safety car came out, and Antonelli pitted under it without losing the lead. Lewis Hamilton was the big loser, held in the pit lane for Verstappen and dropping behind the Mercedes and Piastri.

Run to the flag

Antonelli pulled away at the restart. Behind him, Leclerc got past Hamilton on lap 41 and Russell followed a lap later. Leclerc then held Russell off for the last podium spot, Norris passed Hamilton for fifth, and Pierre Gasly kept Verstappen behind for seventh.

The fallout

Drivers didn't hold back afterwards. Huge speed differences between cars harvesting and cars deploying energy are exactly what made Bearman's crash so dangerous. The FIA opened a review with the engine manufacturers, and changes to the energy rules followed in time for Miami.

My take: Kimi got lucky in this one, the safety car timing was crucial for him.
