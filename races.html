Race Recaps

Every Grand Prix of the season, broken down: what happened, who won, and what it means for the title fight.

Round 16 · Bahrain (Sepang) · October 4

Verstappen Ends His Drought in the Sepang Rain

A rain delay, a string of safety cars and a Mercedes power failure. Max finally gets his first win of 2026.

 Round 15 · Azerbaijan · September 26

Russell Edges Verstappen by Two Tenths in Baku

Two safety cars, a Piastri mistake and a 0.196-second finish. Russell just holds off Verstappen.

 Round 14 · Madrid · September 13

Antonelli Wins the First Race at the Madring

A VSC timed perfectly for Mercedes and a slow McLaren stop hand Antonelli the first win at Madrid's new track.

 Round 13 · Italy · September 6

Antonelli Wins Monza From 19th on the Grid

Gasly takes a shock pole, Leclerc crashes out on lap 2, and Antonelli wins at home from 19th.

 Round 12 · Netherlands · August 23

Norris Makes It Two in a Row at a Wet Zandvoort

Verstappen crashes out of his home race on lap 1, and Norris charges through on fresh tyres to win again.

 Round 11 · Hungary · July 26

Norris Finally Wins in Budapest

Norris finally gets his first win as reigning champion, while Piastri's race falls apart.

 Round 10 · Belgium · July 19

Antonelli Hunts Down Leclerc at Spa

Hamilton takes out Russell on lap 1, then Antonelli reels in Leclerc for the win on the Kemmel Straight.

 Round 9 · Britain · July 5

Leclerc Wins Behind the Safety Car at Silverstone

A Ferrari one-two start, an umbrella on track, and a finish behind the safety car. Leclerc takes his first win of 2026.

 Round 8 · Austria · June 28

Russell Holds Off Verstappen at the Red Bull Ring

The top three cross the line within two seconds as Russell holds on at Red Bull's home track.

 Round 7 · Barcelona · June 14

Hamilton Wins at Last: His First Ferrari Victory

Ferrari gambles on three stops in the heat and it pays off. Hamilton finally wins in red.

 Round 6 · Monaco · June 7

Antonelli Conquers Monaco With a Grand Slam

A red flag, a flood of penalties and a record-breaking grand slam. Antonelli is now the youngest Monaco winner ever.

 Round 5 · Canada · May 24

Antonelli Survives Montreal Mayhem for Four in a Row

Rain, three virtual safety cars and a Russell breakdown. Antonelli wins again, with Hamilton second.

 Round 4 · Miami · May 3

Antonelli Holds Off Norris in Miami for Three Straight

First-lap chaos, an early safety car and a late Norris charge, but Antonelli makes it three wins in a row.

 Round 3 · Japan · March 29

Antonelli Makes It Two as a Scary Crash Sparks a Rules Row

Antonelli wins again at Suzuka, but the big story is Bearman's crash and what it says about the new energy rules.

 Round 2 · China · March 15

Antonelli Takes His First Win in Shanghai

Antonelli goes lights to flag for his first career win, and Hamilton finally gets a Ferrari podium.

 Round 1 · Australia · March 8

Russell Leads a Mercedes One-Two to Open the New Era

The first race of the new era came down to battery boosts, two virtual safety cars and a Ferrari strategy that backfired.
