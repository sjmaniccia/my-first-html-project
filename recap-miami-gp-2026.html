Race Recap · Round 4 · May 3, 2026

Antonelli Holds Off Norris in Miami for Three Straight

The first race under the tweaked energy rules had first-lap chaos, an early safety car, and Kimi Antonelli keeping Lando Norris at bay to the flag.

After a month-long break, with April's Middle East rounds off the calendar because of the conflict in the region, F1 came back in Miami with tweaked energy rules. The result was the same: Kimi Antonelli won again, his third in a row, holding Lando Norris off by 3.3 seconds.

Chaos at Turn 1

Antonelli and Max Verstappen both locked up into the first corner. Verstappen made contact with Charles Leclerc and spun down the order, and Leclerc took the lead. Antonelli got past him on lap 4, but Leclerc went straight back by, and Norris soon moved up to second.

An early safety car

Isack Hadjar hit the wall at the Turn 14 chicane and retired. Then Liam Lawson tried a move on Pierre Gasly, they touched, and Gasly ended up in the barriers. Out came the safety car. Lawson's race ended there too, and Nico Hülkenberg's Audi retired around the same time with overheating.

Norris passed Leclerc for the lead at the restart on lap 11, with Antonelli close behind. Verstappen had pitted under the safety car for hard tyres and dropped to 16th, committing to a very long stint.

Strategy wins it

Leclerc's pit stop was slow and cost him. A few drops of rain fell but never enough for intermediates. When Antonelli, Norris and Piastri pitted around laps 27 to 29, Antonelli came out on top, passing Verstappen, who had been leading on old tyres.

Norris got within a second in the closing laps but couldn't find a way by.

A messy finish

Verstappen's worn tyres left him a sitting duck, and Leclerc and Piastri both got past. Piastri then passed Leclerc for the last podium spot. Russell and Verstappen touched, damaging Russell's front wing.

Leclerc's day got worse. He spun into the wall on the final lap, then got a 22-second penalty after the race for leaving the track, which dropped him from sixth to eighth.
