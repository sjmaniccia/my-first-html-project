Race Recap · Round 7 · June 14, 2026

Hamilton Wins at Last: His First Ferrari Victory

A bold three-stop strategy in scorching heat finally got Lewis Hamilton a win in red, and stopped Antonelli's streak.

It took a while, but it finally happened. Lewis Hamilton won in Barcelona, his first victory for Ferrari and his first win of any kind since 2024. It also ended Kimi Antonelli's five-race winning streak.

Why "Barcelona-Catalunya" Grand Prix? Madrid joined the calendar this year as the new home of the Spanish Grand Prix, so the Barcelona race got a new name.

Hot, hot, hot

George Russell held off Hamilton from pole into Turn 1. Track temperatures were over 50°C, and with Pirelli's softer tyres, wear was the story all afternoon. Charles Leclerc, starting tenth after a qualifying crash, made quick progress through the field.

Ferrari's gamble

Hamilton pitted first around lap 12, and Mercedes responded with Russell a lap later, keeping him ahead. Then on lap 28, Ferrari did something bold: they brought Hamilton in again for mediums, committing him to three stops.

It worked. On fresher tyres, Hamilton was clearly quicker than the Mercedes. When Fernando Alonso stopped on track on lap 41 and brought out a virtual safety car, Ferrari pitted Hamilton a third time, cheaply. He came out ahead of Russell and pulled away to win by nearly 20 seconds.

Late drama

With five laps left, Antonelli passed Russell for second, but damaged his front wing doing it and later retired with an engine problem. Leclerc's race ended with a hydraulics failure, which triggered a second VSC.

History made

Hamilton is now the oldest Grand Prix winner since 1970. With Russell second and Lando Norris third, it was also the first all-British podium since 1968.
