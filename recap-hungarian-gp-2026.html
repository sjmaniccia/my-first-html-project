Race Recap · Round 11 · July 26, 2026

Norris Finally Wins in Budapest

The reigning champion's first win of 2026 came from long stints and strong pace, after an early McLaren squabble.

It's been a tough title defence, but Lando Norris is finally on the board. He won the Hungarian Grand Prix by 15 seconds, his first win as reigning world champion and his first since Abu Dhabi last year.

McLaren vs McLaren

Norris started from pole but ran wide at Turn 2 defending from teammate Oscar Piastri, handing him the lead, and he struggled with the rear of the car on the opening lap.

A McLaren one-two looked on the cards until Piastri clashed with Carlos Sainz at Turn 3. Sainz got a five-second penalty, but Piastri's hopes of winning were gone, and his day ended late in the race with a gearbox failure.

Long stints win it

Norris had the pace to run longer stints than expected, and having fresher tyres late in the race proved decisive. Once he got back in front, he never looked back.

Behind Norris

Max Verstappen finished second, a long way back, and spent much of the race complaining about his car's handling. Kimi Antonelli recovered to third and extended his championship lead.

Charles Leclerc was fourth. Lewis Hamilton crossed the line fourth too, but a five-second penalty for pit lane speeding dropped him to fifth. He'd already started three places back after impeding Piastri in qualifying.

George Russell stalled at the start and recovered to seventh. Both Cadillacs retired.
