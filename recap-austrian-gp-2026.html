Race Recap · Round 8 · June 28, 2026

Russell Holds Off Verstappen at the Red Bull Ring

George Russell kept his nerve with Max Verstappen and Kimi Antonelli closing in, as the top three finished within two seconds.

George Russell won from pole at the Red Bull Ring, but it was tight at the end. Max Verstappen finished just 1.6 seconds behind, and Kimi Antonelli was right on his tail. The top three were covered by less than two seconds.

Verstappen on the move

Russell kept the lead into Turn 1. Lewis Hamilton got past teammate Charles Leclerc early on, and Verstappen passed both Antonelli and Leclerc to climb into third.

Getting past Hamilton was harder. Verstappen's first attempt ended up in the gravel, and he didn't make the move for second stick until around lap 22.

Cadillac brake trouble

Both Cadillacs were out within five laps with overheating brakes. Valtteri Bottas pulled in after a brake fire, and Sergio Pérez stopped in the pit lane with smoke in the cockpit.

VSC luck and strategy

Carlos Sainz stopped on track with an electrical problem, bringing out a virtual safety car. Antonelli had pitted just before it and lost places, while Ferrari put both cars on three-stop strategies that never got them back into the fight.

Antonelli fought back, passing Leclerc on lap 30. Russell pitted on lap 44. Red Bull kept Verstappen out longer to give him fresher tyres for the finish, but that cost him track position. He rejoined about ten seconds behind Russell.

A tense finish

Alex Albon hit a bollard and caused a short VSC on lap 53. Russell ran wide on lap 58 but kept the lead. Verstappen closed on newer tyres but never got close enough to try a pass. Antonelli set the fastest lap and closed in too, but Verstappen held on to second.
