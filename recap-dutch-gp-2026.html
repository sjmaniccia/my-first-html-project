Race Recap · Round 12 · August 23, 2026

Norris Makes It Two in a Row at a Wet Zandvoort

Max Verstappen crashed out on lap 1 at his home race, and Lando Norris used fresher tyres to storm past Antonelli and Hamilton.

Lando Norris has the momentum now. He won at Zandvoort for back-to-back victories, beating Kimi Antonelli by over 11 seconds after a late charge on fresher tyres.

Red Bull had a reshuffled line-up for this one. Isack Hadjar injured his wrist in training and missed the race, so Liam Lawson stepped up from Racing Bulls, with Yuki Tsunoda returning to take Lawson's seat.

Heartbreak for Verstappen

Rain fell just before the race, so it started behind the safety car on dry tyres. Norris kept the lead and Antonelli passed George Russell for second.

Then, at his home race, Max Verstappen lost control on the damp banked final corner and hit the barrier hard. He was unhurt and took responsibility, but the race was red-flagged. Gabriel Bortoleto also spun avoiding the slowing cars.

Antonelli takes over

Antonelli got the better launch at the standing restart on lap 5 and took the lead. Arvid Lindblad and Franco Colapinto were later penalised for overtaking under double yellow flags.

Pit stops shuffled the order several times. Antonelli led through the first round of stops and at half distance, and Lewis Hamilton briefly hit the front when Norris and Antonelli pitted.

Norris's charge

The race was won by staying out. Norris ran a long middle stint and rejoined about five seconds behind Antonelli on fresh hard tyres. He caught him quickly, passed him into Turn 1 on lap 54, then took the lead from Hamilton after Hamilton ran wide at Turn 10.

Late twists

A virtual safety car on lap 55 let Antonelli switch to softs, and Mercedes ordered Russell to let him through on lap 65. But Antonelli's tyres faded, and a second VSC on lap 70, after a collision between the two Williams of Carlos Sainz and Alex Albon, ended any hope of a comeback.
