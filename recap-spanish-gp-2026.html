Race Recap · Round 14 · September 13, 2026

Antonelli Wins the First Race at the Madring

A badly timed virtual safety car and a slow pit stop cost polesitter Lando Norris the win in Madrid's first F1 race in 45 years.

F1 raced in Madrid for the first time in 45 years, at the brand-new Madring street circuit, and Kimi Antonelli became its first winner. It was his eighth win of the season and stretched his championship lead to 81 points.

Norris in control

Polesitter Lando Norris and Antonelli swapped the lead a few times through the opening corners, before Norris settled in and pulled more than six seconds clear.

The VSC that decided it

Then the luck turned. On lap 14, Lance Stroll stopped on track and brought out a virtual safety car. Antonelli, Max Verstappen and George Russell all dived in for cheap pit stops, but Norris had already passed the pit entry and couldn't.

McLaren brought him in on lap 16 for hard tyres, and the stop took around seven seconds. He rejoined fifth, and that was the race gone.

Hard to pass

After the stops, the race was mostly a procession, because overtaking at the Madring turned out to be very difficult. Norris fought back to third behind Antonelli and Verstappen.

Lewis Hamilton's race ended after just six laps with a brake problem. Liam Lawson was sixth for Red Bull, still filling in for the injured Isack Hadjar, and Franco Colapinto took seventh for Alpine.

The title picture

After 14 rounds, Antonelli has 292 points to Russell's 211. It's hard to see anyone stopping him now.
