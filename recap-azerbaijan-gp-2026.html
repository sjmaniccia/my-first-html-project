Race Recap · Round 15 · September 26, 2026

Russell Edges Verstappen by Two Tenths in Baku

Two safety cars wiped out George Russell's lead, but he held off a charging Max Verstappen to the line in a Red Bull double-podium race.

That was close. George Russell won in Baku by 0.196 seconds over Max Verstappen, his third win of the year. Isack Hadjar, back from his wrist injury, completed a double podium for Red Bull.

Red Bull on the charge

Russell kept the lead from pole while Oscar Piastri passed Charles Leclerc for second. Both Red Bulls then got past Leclerc, with Verstappen ahead of Hadjar in third. Red Bull kept them in that order instead of letting Hadjar back by, reportedly to give Verstappen a shot at Piastri and Russell.

Kimi Antonelli started 16th after damaging his suspension in qualifying, and was back in the top ten by lap 17.

Two safety cars

Alex Albon crashed into the barriers, bringing out a safety car that wiped out Russell's lead of around eleven seconds. Russell and Verstappen both switched to softs, while Carlos Sainz lost out, having pitted just before.

Verstappen passed Piastri for second after the restart. Then Franco Colapinto locked up and hit his Alpine teammate Pierre Gasly, who then collided with Lando Norris, bringing out a second safety car.

Piastri's mistake

On lap 40, Piastri locked up at Turn 1 defending from Hadjar and dropped all the way to 15th, which put Hadjar onto the podium.

The finish

Verstappen closed in hard over the final laps, and Russell held him off by less than two tenths. Russell said afterwards he lost power when he lifted for yellow flags after a late crash for Valtteri Bottas.

After the race, Colapinto's unserved penalty became a five-place grid drop for the next race, and Gabriel Bortoleto dropped from 13th to 15th with a penalty for overtaking under yellow flags.
