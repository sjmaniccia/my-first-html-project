Antonelli Takes His First Win in Shanghai

Kimi Antonelli led from pole to the flag for his first Grand Prix victory, while four cars didn't even make the start.

Remember this one. Kimi Antonelli took pole in Shanghai, led every lap from there, and won his first Formula 1 Grand Prix. The Italian teenager became the youngest-ever Grand Prix polesitter in F1 history at 19 years, 6 months, and 17 days old, breaking Sebastian Vettel's long-standing 2008 record. George Russell made it another Mercedes one-two, finishing about five and a half seconds back.

Two races in, Mercedes has a clear edge on everyone else.

A thin grid

The race was strange before it started. Both McLarens failed to start with electrical problems, and so did Gabriel Bortoleto's Audi and Alex Albon's Williams with hydraulic issues. Four cars out before lights out is a bad look for the new power units.

Hamilton beats Leclerc

The best fight was for third. Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc went at it for much of the race, and Hamilton held on. It was his first full Grand Prix podium in red.

Reliability woes

Aston Martin had a miserable day. Lance Stroll retired on lap 9 with a battery problem and Fernando Alonso followed on lap 32 with vibrations. Max Verstappen's Red Bull also went out, on lap 45, with a coolant leak.

Oliver Bearman quietly put the Haas fifth, best of the rest behind the top two teams, and Antonelli capped his day with the fastest lap.
