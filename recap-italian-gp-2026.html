Race Recap · Round 13 · September 6, 2026

Antonelli Wins Monza From 19th on the Grid

A shock Alpine pole, a lap 2 red flag, and a home hero who came from nearly last to become the first Italian to win at Monza in 60 years.

Write this one down. Kimi Antonelli started 19th at Monza and won. He's the first driver to win from that far back since John Watson in 1983, and the first Italian to win the Italian Grand Prix since 1966. The tifosi might have been cheering for the wrong team, but they got an Italian winner.

A shock pole

Pierre Gasly started from pole, the first of his career and the first ever for Alpine as a team. He led the opening lap before George Russell got by on lap 2.

Ferrari heartbreak

Ferrari's home race was over for Charles Leclerc almost immediately. On lap 2, he ran over a drainage grate at the Parabolica, spun and hit the barrier. Repairing it took about half an hour under a red flag.

The restart was messy too. Yuki Tsunoda slid in his grid box and the first attempt was aborted. Franco Colapinto had climbed to third before the stoppage, but went off at the restart and lost a lot of places.

Antonelli takes charge

Antonelli carved through the field and took the lead on lap 18. Russell got it back on lap 23. Fernando Alonso retired on lap 25, and when Lance Stroll stopped at the first chicane on lap 28, the virtual safety car came out.

That decided it. Antonelli and Max Verstappen, both within two seconds of Russell, pitted under the VSC along with several others. Antonelli came out on top and held on to win by under four seconds, with Verstappen third. Gasly finished seventh.

Red Bull line-up

Isack Hadjar was still out with his wrist injury, so Liam Lawson drove for Red Bull again, finishing 14th.
